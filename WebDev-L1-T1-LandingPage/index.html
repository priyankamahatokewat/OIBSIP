Why our tea tastes different

Cool nights, morning mist and slow growth give Ilam leaves a delicate flavour that large estates struggle to match.

Bought from the grower

We pay farmers directly and publish the price per kilo, so the people who pick the leaf keep the larger share.

Small batches only

Each batch is limited to what one garden produces in a week. When it sells out, we wait for the next harvest.

Sealed the same day

Leaves are dried, graded and packed within 24 hours of rolling, locking in aroma that fades in open storage.
